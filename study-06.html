Week 06 · The Press

From Pressing to Pouring: Wholeness

The closing study in the series, focused on what God has formed through the pressing and how to carry that fruit into the next season.

Main Scriptures

Isaiah 61 · Revelation 21:5

Focus

Healing, restoration, and becoming a vessel that pours out what God has done within.

Study Overview

God does not waste the pressing. He restores, renews, and often turns the places of pain into places of testimony and ministry. Isaiah 61 shows the exchange God delights to make, and Revelation 21 lifts our eyes to His final work of renewal. This final study is about recognizing fruit, receiving healing, and moving forward with gratitude and purpose.

Read and Reflect

"He has sent me to bind up the brokenhearted... to bestow on them a crown of beauty instead of ashes."

  1. What fruit has God produced in you through this season?
  2. Where do you sense Him bringing restoration or renewal?
  3. How might your story become a source of comfort or hope for someone else?

Prayer Prompt

God, thank You for not leaving me unchanged. Heal what is still tender, confirm what You have grown in me, and help me pour out to others what You have faithfully poured into me.

Action Step

Write a short testimony paragraph finishing this sentence: "Through this season, God has shown me..." Keep it as a reminder of His faithfulness.
