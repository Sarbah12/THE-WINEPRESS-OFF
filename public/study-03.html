Week 03 · The Press

Waiting Well: Faith in the In-Between

A study for the long middle season, when you have a promise, but you do not yet have the outcome.

Main Scriptures

Habakkuk 2:1-3 · Psalm 27:14

Focus

Patience, expectancy, and learning to stand watch in faith.

Study Overview

Waiting can expose what quick answers often hide. It brings our anxieties, timelines, and need for control to the surface. Habakkuk shows a posture of watchfulness rather than passivity, and Psalm 27 anchors waiting in courage. This week is about learning how to remain spiritually awake while you wait, rather than becoming cynical, numb, or self-protective.

Read and Reflect

"Wait for the Lord; be strong and take heart and wait for the Lord."

  1. What makes this current waiting season hard for you?
  2. How can you remain spiritually attentive instead of emotionally shutting down?
  3. What would courageous waiting look like for you right now?

Prayer Prompt

God, steady my heart while I wait. Keep me from drifting into fear or despair. Teach me to watch, pray, and trust You with the timing of what You have promised.

Action Step

Choose one promise from Scripture and write it somewhere visible. Return to it every day this week and answer, "What does faithfulness look like today while I wait?"
